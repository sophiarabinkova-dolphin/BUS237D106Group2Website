Agentic AI in Banking:
Transforming the Financial Industry

Exploring how agentic AI is reshaping customer service, banking operations, employment, and the future of financial services.

Executive overview

Agentic AI is moving banking beyond basic task automation toward systems that can plan and complete multi-step work with limited human supervision.

Banks can use these systems to improve customer service, fraud review, mortgage processing, and operational efficiency. The value is significant, but autonomy must be matched with privacy protection, human judgment, and clear accountability.

01

Faster service

Handle routine inquiries, reduce waiting, and resolve common customer needs around the clock.

02

Operational efficiency

Coordinate data and tasks across systems to reduce manual work and processing time.

03

Fraud protection

Review suspicious transactions, check spending history, and recommend when action is needed.

01 · The technology

AI that can pursue a goal—not just answer a prompt.

Instead of simply responding to instructions, agentic AI can decide what to do next and complete a task with minimal human supervision.

An agentic system receives a task, creates a plan, gathers the information it needs, checks the results, and takes action or escalates when human judgment is required.

01

Traditional automation

Follows fixed rules for predictable and repetitive work.

02

Chatbots and generative AI

Respond or create content, then wait for the next instruction.

03

Agentic AI

Coordinates multiple steps, adjusts when information changes, takes action, and escalates exceptions.

TRANSFORMING CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

Move from answering to resolving.

An AI agent can remember context, detect risks such as an impending overdraft, recommend a solution in real time, and resolve multi-step issues without placing the customer on hold. Speed creates convenience, but empathy and human judgment build long-term trust.

Reduce handoffs, duplicate work, delays, and avoidable cost.

REAL-WORLD CASE STUDY

TD Layer 6

TD uses Layer 6 for mortgage pre-adjudication. It reads customer tax forms, pay stubs, and bank statements, replacing manual document scanning, calculations, and policy checks. The presentation reports that processing time fell from 15 hours to 3 minutes.

OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCY

Vendor platforms

Pre-built agentic engines from enterprise vendors such as Microsoft can connect to existing bank databases and support work such as fraud investigations. Bain & Company estimates a 20–30% productivity increase across core operational functions.

FINANCIAL INSTITUTION

JPMorgan Chase

JPMorgan is developing connected AI agents for corporate cash and treasury work. They gather and analyze company data and prepare recommendations, while humans retain oversight before important financial decisions.

TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM

Salesforce Agentforce

Agentforce provides a platform for building and deploying agents that use company data and complete multi-step tasks. Greater independence also creates privacy and reliability concerns that banks must manage.

MANAGEMENT IMPACT

AI prepares and recommends; people make the final decision.

Managers remain fully accountable for AI outputs, regulatory compliance, and potential bias—especially in lending. They must protect customer data, verify the accuracy of information, train staff properly, and decide when human approval is required.

IMPLEMENTATION PRINCIPLES

Autonomy must be matched with accountability.

Accurate

Validate information and monitor errors.

Fair

Test for bias in lending and service.

Secure

Protect customer data and limit access.

Human-led

Keep final authority with accountable employees.

Risks & costs

Bias, cyber risk, privacy exposure, integration failures, regulatory requirements, vendor dependence, and unclear accountability can offset promised productivity.

Human oversight

Approval thresholds, audit trails, agent monitoring, escalation paths, named accountability, and regulatory compliance should be designed before deployment.

Future operating model

A single employee may eventually oversee a team of specialized AI “coworkers.” Banks should introduce this model gradually, define decision rights, and help employees adapt.

04 · Employment & future of work

Human-and-agent teams are the future operating model.

A future “10x bank” could allow one employee to set goals and review decisions while several AI coworkers analyze data, recommend actions, and execute approved tasks.

EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITY

Set goals and review decisions

  • Define roles for people and AI
  • Set human approval thresholds
  • Retain judgment and accountability

Redesign work gradually.

Banks should clearly assign tasks, decide when human sign-off is required, and give employees time and training to adapt before expanding the model. Agentic AI may change task composition and job requirements, but human relationships, empathy, ethics, and oversight remain central.
